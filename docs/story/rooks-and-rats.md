# Rooks and Rats: the farm job

The job that puts a rifle in Askew's hands, written from James's brief of 21 October 2026: *he finds a farm, speaks to the owner, who asks him to take out some corvids and rats on his farm; if he can get them all he can keep the rifle; the farmer then gives him the contact of someone with potentially more work in a pub further down the canal.* It is built and playable; it sits beside the demo's six missions until its place in the order is settled.

## Where

**Dunstall Farm**, on the west bank of the Birmingham and Fazeley pound, 1,750 metres south of Hopwas bridge, the first farm the map names below Hopwas. The stackyard stands forty-odd metres back from the water with its gate towards the cut: a post-and-rail fence, two thatched hayricks, a muck heap. Rooks and crows sit on the ricks and the posts and shift between them; a magpie works the heap; rats run between the rick feet and the muck. Askew lies *Sparrow* against the off-side bank, steps ashore and walks up to the gate.

## Who

**Pryce**, the farmer, stands inside the gate and turns to watch a man who comes up from the water. He is not in the demo's cast and need not outlive this job.

## How it plays

1. **The offer.** Within a few yards of Pryce the prompt reads *Talk to the farmer*. He says: *You off the boat? Thought so. Nobody else walks up here.* Then: *I've rooks in the stackyard and rats under the ricks, and a rook rifle I've no time to use.* Then: *Clear the lot, every one of them, and the rifle's yours. Cartridges are in the box by the gate.* The rifle is in Askew's hand when the talk ends, carried at the trail, and the card at the top of the screen reads *Rooks and rats: 0 of 10*.
2. **The cull.** Hold the right mouse button (or the left trigger) to shoulder the rifle: the camera closes in over his shoulder, a ring sits in the middle of the screen, the mouse lays the aim. The left button (or the right trigger) fires. A single-shot rook rifle: one crack, the bolt worked, a second and a half before the next. Six corvids (three rooks, two crows, a magpie) and four rats. A bird shot off a rick drops to the yard. The count climbs on the card; when the last falls it reads *All of them. Tell the farmer.* Talk to Pryce before then and he says *Not yet. I can still hear them.*
3. **The rifle and the lead.** Pryce: *Every one of them. I've not seen shooting like that since France.* *Keep the rifle. You'll want it where you're going.* *There's a man drinks at the Three Tuns at Fazeley, name of Holland, agent at Tolson's mill. He asked after a man who could shoot.* *Ring Fazeley 14 and say Pryce sent you. Further down the cut, past the junction. You can't miss the mill.* The card now carries the lead and keeps it: **Holland · the Three Tuns, Fazeley · Fazeley 14**.

## What it hands on

Holland is the mill agent of [characters.md](characters.md), the bank-sider with work for a man who can find things, so the lead points at the demo's Fazeley end. The number is a 1938 exchange number: the town's name and two figures, the kind a farmer would read off a card by the telephone.

## The gun it gives

A rook rifle: the farmer's gun of the period, a light single-shot sporting rifle for rooks and rabbits. It is the basic rifle, not the scoped one. The rifle is carried in the open while Askew is ashore and stowed below when he steps aboard; it is not yet the hidden weapon of [systems.md](systems.md), and the pound does not yet hear the shot.

## What is not settled

[protagonist.md](protagonist.md) has Askew owning three guns already, and the demo's first mission teaches the rifle on the range. This job gives him his first rifle, so one of the two has to give: either this is the job before the demo and the locker is emptier than written, or it is the job that gives him the rook rifle he carries openly while the real guns stay hidden. That is James's call.
