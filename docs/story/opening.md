# The cold open

*The Pound* opens like this. Black screen. A blowlamp roaring.

Inside the engine hole of *Halcyon*. Askew's hands, the lamp playing on the hot-bulb of the Bolinder, the cabin lamp swinging. He counts under his breath. Kicks the flywheel. The engine catches, misses, catches, and settles into the slow single beat that will be the sound of the whole game. He wipes his hands. Climbs out.

Morning on the cut at Hopwas. Mist on the water, the Tame low and brown beyond the towpath, the wood above the village a wall of turning oak. He looks up. Red flags on the poles. The range is live. The first shots come down the hill, flat and unhurried, a squad on the butts. He listens to them the way another man would listen to a clock.

The player has the tiller. The pound is theirs to the chain at Fazeley and the planks at Huddlesford. A picket on the towpath at Hopwas bridge raises a hand. Askew raises one back.

At the Tame Otter, Nell is scrubbing the step. She says the sergeant-major was in last night asking after him, and did not drink, which is not like him. Askew says nothing. She says the strike is going into its sixth week and the company has not sent a man to talk, which is not like them either. Askew says nothing to that too.

He walks up through the wood on the path that is not on the range map, to the firing point, where Garner is standing behind a squad of boys who cannot hit the target at two hundred yards. Garner does not turn round. He says, into the wind, that a lad called Rudd went over the wall on Tuesday with his kit and a rifle that was not his, and that the colonel wants him back in the guardroom by Sunday and nothing in the county paper, and that the army will pay what the army pays.

Askew asks which rifle.

Garner says that is the thing. The book says it never existed.

The flags come down. Askew walks back to the boat. On the cabin side, under the soot, the name *Halcyon* and the roses. In the locker, the scope. The engine is still running. He never turned it off.

Title card. **SHALLOW WATER.** Then, smaller: *The Pound.*
