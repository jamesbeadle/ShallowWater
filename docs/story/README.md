# Shallow Water — the story bible

This folder is the source of truth for the story of Shallow Water. Every game in the series is a chapter of one story, and every chapter is written from these pages. The design brief (working title *The Cut*) fixed the time, the place and the man; these pages expand inside that fence and never move it.

## The fence

- 1934 to 1938, default 1936. No Victorian golden age, no 1950s leisure, no war blackout until a chapter earns it.
- The demo is the water from Huddlesford Junction to Fazeley Junction, plus what can be walked from it: Hopwas Hays Wood, the barracks approach, Fazeley's streets.
- Hopwas village and Hopwas Hays Wood are one home with two textures.
- The player is a loner who takes bounty and recovery work. Not a copper, not a gang boss, not a company man, not a commando.
- A scoped rifle is a veteran's gun and a social problem, never a power fantasy.
- The junctions are shut by things a person can see and, in Game 1, remove.
